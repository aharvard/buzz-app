# Membership

Invite people to your community and manage their roles and access.

The plugin is on by default; **Settings → Plugins → Manage plugins** can turn it off.

Membership appears under **Community admin** in Settings when you select a
community where you are an owner or admin.

## Getting started

1. Go to **Settings → Community admin → Membership**.
2. Choose **Invite members** to add someone or copy an invite link.
3. Use a member's menu to manage their role or remove access, according to your permissions.

## Welcome channel

On relays that support it, **Settings → Community admin → Welcome** lets an
owner or admin choose the public channel new members join and open first, and
the title and message shown at the start of that channel. The message editor
has the composer's formatting toolbar and saves Markdown. Saving moves
the welcome to the chosen channel; people already in the community keep their
channels.

Below that, **Also add new members to** lists the starter channels, which new
members join too: **Add a channel** searches other open channels, and **Remove**
takes one off. These edit the same draft as the welcome: **Save** publishes
the welcome first, then each starter change, and **Cancel** discards them.
Saved changes apply to people who join afterwards. The list, with its Save,
also appears on relays without a welcome channel.
