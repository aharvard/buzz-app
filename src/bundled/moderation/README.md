# Membership

Invite people to your community and manage their roles and access.

The plugin is on by default; **Settings → Plugins → Manage plugins** can turn it off.

Membership appears under **Community admin** in Settings when you select a
community where you are an owner or admin.

## Getting started

1. Go to **Settings → Community admin → Membership**.
2. Choose **Invite members** to add someone or copy an invite link.
3. Use a member's menu to manage their role or remove access, according to your permissions.

## Welcome channel

On relays that support it, **Settings → Community admin → Welcome** lets an
owner or admin choose the public channel new members join and open first, and
the message shown at the start of that channel. Saving moves the welcome to the
chosen channel; people already in the community keep their channels.
